# Quality Checklist

Review every generated or edited image. A failed hard gate requires a targeted correction before delivery.

## A. Badge artwork

### Identity — hard gate

- [ ] The result is the same person shown in the current references, not a generic design based only on a character name.
- [ ] Hair shape, color, fringe, face shape, and visible facial marks are correct.
- [ ] Eye color, open or closed state, gaze, and expression are correct.
- [ ] Clothing construction, palette, damage, accessories, weapon, and key action are correct.
- [ ] Body type and apparent age have not drifted or been exaggerated without evidence.

### Ownership and anatomy — hard gate

- [ ] No hand, leg, sleeve, weapon, or partial body from another character has been assigned to the subject.
- [ ] No extra limbs, duplicate weapons, fused anatomy, or detached parts are present.
- [ ] Visible finger count and connections are plausible; thumb direction matches palm orientation.
- [ ] Arms connect naturally to shoulders; neck, torso, waist, hips, and legs remain proportionate.
- [ ] Every limb remains traceable through connection and occlusion, including under strong foreshortening.

### Composition and finish

- [ ] Canvas is 1:1; circular crop reads clearly and the background fills the circle edge.
- [ ] The subject is dominant and has not been made too small to preserve a full-body view.
- [ ] Face, head, key gesture, and identity accessories are not incorrectly cropped.
- [ ] Background does not cover the face, damage the silhouette, or create ownership ambiguity.
- [ ] No unintended text, name, logo, watermark, platform overlay, studio mark, or decorative gold rim appears.
- [ ] The result reads as a polished anime illustration, not figure photography or low-quality AI output.

## B. Physical badge mockup

### Artwork consistency — hard gate

- [ ] The inserted design is the approved artwork version.
- [ ] Face, gesture, clothing, background elements, and relative layout have not been redrawn.
- [ ] Only surface-related crop, perspective, lighting, reflection, and edge occlusion have changed.

### Photographic realism — hard gate

- [ ] Metal rim, thickness, dome, and silhouette remain physically convincing.
- [ ] Artwork follows camera perspective and surface curvature rather than appearing pasted on.
- [ ] Original highlights, reflections, shadows, and contact are preserved above the art.
- [ ] Background texture, grain, exposure, and color temperature have not drifted without reason.
- [ ] Artwork stays inside the printable face and does not cover the metal rim.

## C. Delivery

- [ ] The full workflow contains both artwork and physical mockup unless the user requested one stage.
- [ ] Character, clothing, action, and design correspond exactly across both deliverables.
- [ ] Results are clearly distinguishable as `01-badge-art` and `02-badge-mockup`.
- [ ] A visual design is not described as production-ready without vendor specifications.

## Correction strategy

1. Repair hard gates before composition or decoration.
2. Describe one observable defect per correction, such as “remove the upper-right hand that does not belong to the subject.”
3. Preserve identity anchors and layout that are already correct.
4. Stop after two automatic correction rounds per stage and disclose any remaining hard-gate defect.
